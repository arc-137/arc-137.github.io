Drawing walls and rooms

  • Wall: click the start, then each corner. Right-click, double-click or Esc to stop. Click the first corner to close a room.
  • Rooms are found by themselves when walls close a space. Select one to name it and set its floor and ceiling.
  • Drag a wall to push it; drag a blue grip to move an end.
  • Snap ▾: angle lock, Snap to walls, Round to, ft-in or mm. Hold Shift to free the angle for a moment.

Doors and windows

  • Hover over a wall to see the distance to each end; click to place.
  • The door swings to the side your cursor is on; F flips the hinge.
  • Several at once (panel): set How many, then Equal sections, Equal gaps, Centred on the wall, From the end, or From where I click. One click places them all.

Selecting

  • Click to select; the panel on the right shows its settings.
  • Drag a box left → right: things fully inside. Right → left: anything it touches.
  • Nothing selected: the panel shows the Project and the Design check.

Getting help

  • Ctrl+K: type what you want to do (stairs, pdf, sofa).
  • ? on a panel heading opens the guide at that topic.
  • Guide ▾ on the top bar: every video lesson, the hands-on lessons and the user guide.
  • Guide ▾ → Hands-on lessons…: build a small house step by step.
  • Video lessons: short videos, start to finish.
  • File ▾ → Samples…: finished plans to explore.
Design check warnings: Show finds it, Ignore sets it aside, Note keeps your reason with the plan. Code checks are a guide for early design; a licensed professional and the building official have the final say.
